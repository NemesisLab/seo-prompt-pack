# 🚀 The Viral Social Media Prompt Pack
*12 Battle-Tested Prompts to Skyrocket Your Engagement in 2025*

## 📱 INSTAGRAM & TIKTOK

### 1. Viral Hook Generator
**Use this when:** You need an attention-grabbing first line for Reels/TikToks

**Prompt:**
> "Generate 10 viral hook ideas for a [PLATFORM: Instagram Reel/TikTok] about [TOPIC]. Each hook should:
> - Start with a pattern interrupt (shocking statement, question, or bold claim)
> - Be under 8 words
> - Create curiosity or FOMO
> - Appeal to [TARGET AUDIENCE]
> 
> Format: Numbered list with a brief explanation of why each hook works."

**Example Output:**
1. "Stop scrolling. This changed everything." → Creates urgency + curiosity
2. "Nobody talks about this..." → Implies secret knowledge

---

### 2. Reel/Short Script Writer (30-60 seconds)
**Use this when:** You need a complete video script

**Prompt:**
> "Write a 45-second [Instagram Reel/TikTok/YouTube Short] script about [TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE].
> 
> Structure:
> - **Hook (0-3s):** Attention-grabbing opening
> - **Problem (3-10s):** What pain point does this solve?
> - **Solution (10-40s):** 3 quick tips or steps
> - **CTA (40-45s):** What should they do next?
> 
> Tone: [casual/professional/funny/inspirational]
> Include suggested on-screen text overlays in [brackets]."

---

### 3. Carousel Post Creator
**Use this when:** You want to create educational "swipe-through" posts

**Prompt:**
> "Create a 10-slide Instagram carousel about [TOPIC] that teaches [TARGET AUDIENCE] how to [GOAL].
> 
> For each slide, provide:
> - **Slide Title** (max 6 words, bold/eye-catching)
> - **Body Text** (2-3 short sentences, easy to read on mobile)
> - **Visual Suggestion** (what image/graphic to use)
> 
> Slide 1 must be a hook that makes people want to swipe.
> Slide 10 must include a clear CTA."

## 🐦 TWITTER/X

### 4. Viral Thread Builder
**Use this when:** You want to turn any idea into a high-engagement thread

**Prompt:**
> "Turn this idea into a viral Twitter/X thread: [YOUR IDEA/TOPIC]
> 
> Requirements:
> - 8-12 tweets total
> - Tweet 1: Hook that promises value (e.g., '10 things I wish I knew about...')
> - Tweets 2-10: Numbered insights, each with a mini-story or example
> - Tweet 11: Summary + CTA
> - Use line breaks for readability
> - Include 2-3 strategic emoji per tweet (not excessive)
> 
> Optimize for retweets and bookmarks."

---

### 5. Engagement Reply Generator
**Use this when:** You want to boost your replies to get noticed

**Prompt:**
> "I want to reply to this tweet: '[PASTE TWEET]'
> 
> Generate 5 different reply options that:
> - Add value (insight, joke, or helpful tip)
> - Are 1-2 sentences max
> - Increase the chance of the original poster engaging back
> - Don't sound spammy or self-promotional
> 
> Tone: [witty/supportive/thought-provoking]"

## 🎯 CROSS-PLATFORM

### 6. Content Repurposing Machine
**Use this when:** You want to turn one piece of content into 10+

**Prompt:**
> "I have this content: [PASTE BLOG POST/VIDEO TRANSCRIPT/PODCAST NOTES]
> 
> Repurpose it into:
> 1. A Twitter/X thread (10 tweets)
> 2. 3 Instagram carousel topics (just the titles)
> 3. 5 LinkedIn post ideas
> 4. 3 TikTok/Reel hooks
> 5. 1 email subject line + preview text
> 
> Keep the core message but adapt the tone for each platform."

---

### 7. Viral Challenge Creator
**Use this when:** You want to start a trend or challenge

**Prompt:**
> "Create a viral challenge concept for [PLATFORM] related to [NICHE/TOPIC].
> 
> Include:
> - **Challenge Name** (catchy, uses a hashtag)
> - **How It Works** (simple, 3 steps max)
> - **Why It Will Go Viral** (psychological trigger: FOMO, fun, social proof, etc.)
> - **Example Post Caption**
> - **Suggested Hashtags** (3-5)"

---

### 8. Storytelling Post Formula
**Use this when:** You want to share a personal story that resonates

**Prompt:**
> "Help me write a storytelling post about [EXPERIENCE/LESSON LEARNED] for [PLATFORM].
> 
> Use this structure:
> - **Hook:** Start in the middle of the action or with a bold statement
> - **Setup:** Briefly set the scene (who, what, when, where)
> - **Conflict:** What went wrong or what challenge did you face?
> - **Resolution:** How did you overcome it?
> - **Lesson:** What's the takeaway for the reader?
> - **CTA:** Ask a question to spark comments
> 
> Tone: Vulnerable but inspiring. Max 200 words."

## 💬 ENGAGEMENT BOOSTERS

### 9. Comment Magnet Questions
**Use this when:** You need more comments on your posts

**Prompt:**
> "Generate 15 engaging questions I can ask my audience about [TOPIC/NICHE].
> 
> Requirements:
> - Easy to answer (no essay required)
> - Sparks debate or personal sharing
> - Mix of 'This or That', 'Fill in the Blank', and open-ended
> - Avoids yes/no questions
> 
> Example: 'What's one [TOPIC] myth you used to believe?'"

---

### 10. Controversy Creator (Use Wisely!)
**Use this when:** You want to spark conversation with a hot take

**Prompt:**
> "Generate 5 'spicy but defensible' hot takes about [TOPIC/INDUSTRY] that will spark engagement.
> 
> Each hot take should:
> - Challenge conventional wisdom
> - Be backed by logic (not just rage-bait)
> - Invite people to agree OR disagree (both are engagement)
> - Be stated confidently but not offensively
> 
> Format: '[Hot Take]' + 1-sentence reasoning."

## 📊 ANALYTICS & OPTIMIZATION

### 11. Caption A/B Test Generator
**Use this when:** You want to test different caption styles

**Prompt:**
> "I'm posting about [TOPIC]. Generate 3 different caption styles for A/B testing:
> 
> **Version A:** Storytelling (personal anecdote)
> **Version B:** Educational (quick tips/facts)
> **Version C:** Provocative (bold statement + question)
> 
> Each caption should:
> - Be 100-150 words
> - Include 3-5 relevant hashtags
> - End with a clear CTA
> 
> Tell me which version you predict will perform best and why."

## 🎁 BONUS: The 'Viral Formula' Cheat Sheet

**Every viral post has 3 elements:**
1. **Hook:** Pattern interrupt (first 3 seconds/words)
2. **Value:** Teach, entertain, or inspire
3. **CTA:** Tell them what to do next (comment, share, follow)
